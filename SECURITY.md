## Reporting a Vulnerability or Safety Issue

Since Ignis Drive is a DIY analog hardware project, "security" primarily refers to electrical safety and mechanical reliability. 

If you discover a critical design flaw (e.g., a severe short circuit risk, component overheating potential, or a reversed polarity issue in the PCB files), please do not open a public Issue immediately. Instead, please report it directly to the maintainer via email to ensure it is addressed before others manufacture the faulty board.

**Contact:** bkus.business@gmail.com

All safety-critical reports will be addressed as quickly as possible, and the PCB files will be updated with a formal release patch.
